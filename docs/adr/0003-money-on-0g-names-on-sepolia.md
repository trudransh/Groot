# Money and decisions live on the 0G testnet, names live on Sepolia

0G's tracks require the decision loop, the decision records and the contracts on 0G (Galileo testnet, chain 16602). ENS v2 is deployed on Ethereum mainnet and Sepolia only. So plant wallets, the forest contract and water orders are on 0G, and the forest's names and records are on Sepolia. Each plant name carries its wallet address as an ENSIP-11 address record for chain 16602, which is how the app goes from a name to a wallet without hard-coded addresses.
