# 0G: how the forest uses it

Tracks: Physical AI (main), Wildcard.

## What breaks without 0G

Without 0G a plant has no mind and no memory: nothing decides when it needs water, and nothing proves that the pump only ever ran on a decision that followed the forest's rules.

## Must-shows

| 0G call inside the decision loop | 0G Router (mainnet Compute), `agent/src/decider/`, Groot's inner loop in `groot/agent/voice_agent/inner_loop.py` |
| Decision record per decision on 0G Storage, with request ID | `agent/src/live/storage.ts`; record `0xab76e2f2dac9403ff33881d22dc0c0a343620f17709343d05ed06815c18f5624` on Galileo |
